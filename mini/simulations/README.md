# simulations

Small numeric simulations, standard library only.

- `bouncing_balls.py` — elastic balls in a box, reports energy conservation.
- `predator_prey.py` — Lotka-Volterra populations drawn as sparklines.
- `epidemic_sir.py` — SIR epidemic model with a text bar chart.
- `traffic_nasch.py` — Nagel-Schreckenberg ring-road traffic with phantom jams.
- `random_walk_stats.py` — 2D random walkers; mean squared displacement grows linearly.
- `gravity_orbit.py` — leapfrog planet orbit with ASCII plot and energy drift.
- `pendulum.py` — RK4 pendulum; measured period vs the small-angle formula.
- `game_of_life_density.py` — Conway's Life on a torus; final density vs initial density.
- `diffusion_limited_aggregation.py` — DLA random-walker cluster drawn in ASCII.
- `ising_model.py` — 2D Ising Metropolis simulation; magnetisation collapses near T_c ≈ 2.27.
- `sandpile.py` — Abelian sandpile toppling drawn in ASCII.
- `schelling_segregation.py` — Schelling model: mild preferences produce strong segregation.
