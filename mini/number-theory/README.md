# number-theory

Small number-theory toys, standard library only.

- `sieve_spiral.py` — Ulam spiral of primes in ASCII (`python sieve_spiral.py 31`).
- `modular_toolkit.py` — extended gcd, modular inverse, Chinese remainder theorem with self-checks.
- `collatz_stats.py` — longest Collatz chain below N plus a stopping-time histogram.
- `perfect_numbers.py` — perfect/abundant/deficient counts and amicable pairs via a divisor-sum sieve.
- `totient_table.py` — Euler's totient sieve checked against brute force.
- `goldbach.py` — verifies Goldbach's conjecture up to N and finds the number with most decompositions.
- `legendre_sqrt_mod.py` — Legendre symbol and Tonelli-Shanks modular square roots.
- `pollard_rho.py` — Pollard rho factoring with Miller-Rabin.
- `continued_fractions.py` — continued fraction expansions, convergents, sqrt periods.
- `lucas_lehmer.py` — Lucas-Lehmer test for Mersenne primes and the perfect numbers they give.
- `quadratic_residues.py` — squares mod n, primitive roots and an Euler-criterion check.
- `digital_root.py` — digital roots, multiplicative persistence and Harshad numbers.
