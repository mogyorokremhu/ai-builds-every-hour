# probability

Probability puzzles checked by simulation, each self-contained with a built-in check.

- `birthday_paradox.py` — exact vs. simulated chance of a shared birthday.
- `monty_hall.py` — simulates staying vs. switching doors.
- `gamblers_ruin.py` — exact and simulated ruin probability for fair and biased coins.
- `coupon_collector.py` — expected draws to collect every coupon (n·H(n)) vs. simulation.
- `buffon_needle.py` — estimates pi by dropping virtual needles.
- `random_walk.py` — 1-D walk: mean distance √(2n/π) and return-to-origin chance.
- `secretary_problem.py` — optimal stopping: skip n/e candidates, win ~37% of the time.
- `bayes_disease_test.py` — base-rate fallacy: exact Bayes posterior vs. simulated screening.
- `dice_sum_distribution.py` — sum-of-dice distribution by convolution vs. simulation.
