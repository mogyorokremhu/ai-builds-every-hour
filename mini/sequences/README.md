# sequences

Famous integer sequences, each self-contained with built-in asserts.

- `collatz.py` — Collatz chains and the longest chain below a limit.
- `look_and_say.py` — look-and-say terms and Conway's growth ratio.
- `recaman.py` — Recamán's sequence and its first repeated value.
- `thue_morse.py` — Thue-Morse terms by popcount parity and substitution, cube-free check.
- `zeckendorf.py` — unique non-consecutive Fibonacci decompositions of integers.
- `pascal_mod.py` — Pascal's triangle mod m, Lucas' theorem check, Sierpinski rendering.
