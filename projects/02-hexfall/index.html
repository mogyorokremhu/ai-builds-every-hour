      Click one of your units, then a yellow hex to move or a red-ringed enemy to attack (it steps next to melee targets by itself). Hover an enemy for the damage forecast. Keys: E end turn, U undo move, N/Tab next unit, Esc deselect. Wheel zooms, drag pans. Entering a hex next to an enemy (its zone of control) ends your move.
