      Click one of your units, then a yellow hex to move or a red-ringed enemy to attack (it steps next to melee targets by itself). Hover an enemy for the damage forecast. Keys: E end turn, U undo move, N/Tab next unit, Esc deselect. Wheel zooms, drag pans. Entering a hex next to an enemy (its zone of control) ends your move. Moving onto a village captures it (+2 gold per turn); recruit on the green castle hexes around your keep. Lawful units fight 25% better by day, chaotic ones by night. Every unit has an ability (buttons under the economy panel, keys 1–9): charge, volley, fireball, heal, shield wall, rally, envenom — purple rings mark their targets. Rain dulls arrows and douses fires, fog shortens sight and range.
